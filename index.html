Why Guitar?

FAQs

All you need is your own guitar, a plectrum and the enthusiasm to learn! Worksheets, envelope folder and other materials used throughout the lessons will be provided.

Each lesson is 60 minutes long. The lesson plans are broken down into manageable 15 minute sections throughout each lesson, to keep kids engaged and ensuring each child gets the right balance of time and attention.

A term is 12 weeks long. This is a total of twelve 1-hour weekly group lessons.

Although the lessons are designed for complete beginners, the lesson plans are designed for students attending 3rd to 6th Class. So, the age limit for these classes is no younger than 8 years old and no older than 12 or 13 depending on the age at 6th Class.

Yes! To give the best time and attention to each child and ensure they get the most out of learning guitar, the class size is kept small.

Absolutely! Siblings can book together, and will receive a discounted rate for two or more children from the same family. Please get in touch for more information.

Refunds can't be offerred, but if an absence is notified in advance, arrangements can be made on availability to ensure that your child doesn't fall behind for the lesson that they missed. Please notify as soon as possible if your child is unable to attend a weekly lesson.

Just send an email or a message via WhatsApp or text, to get started. Please provide the number of kids interested in guitar lessons if you have multiple children at home who are interested, their age, and any questions that you may have.
