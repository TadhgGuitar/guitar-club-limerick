Welcome!

Hello, and welcome! I'm Tadhg, and I am the owner of Limerick Guitar Club. As someone who has been playing the guitar for over 20 years, I know that the key to learning is all about being engaged and having fun through the process!

With this in mind, my lessons are interactive, easy to follow and help your child get a sense of achievement at the end, while learning a new skill.

FAQs

All you need is your own guitar, an electronic tuner and the enthusiasm to learn! Worksheets, plectrum(s), envelope folder and other materials used throughout the lessons will be provided.

Each lesson is 60 minutes long. The lesson plans are broken down into manageable 15 minute sections throughout each lesson, to keep kids engaged and ensuring each child gets the right balance of time and attention.

A term is 10 weeks long. This is a total of twelve 1-hour weekly group lessons.

The lesson plans are designed for students attending 3rd to 6th Class. So, the age limit for these classes is no younger than 8 years old and no older than 12 or 13 depending on the age at 6th Class.

Yes! To give the best time and attention to each child and ensure they get the most out of learning guitar, the class size is kept small.

Absolutely! Siblings can book together, and will receive a discounted rate for two or more children from the same household. Please get in touch for more information.

Refunds can't be offerred. Please get in touch as soon as possible if your child will be absent from a lesson. Depending on time and availability, best efforts can be made to help the child catch up from the lesson they missed.
